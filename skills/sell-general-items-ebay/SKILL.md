# Sell general items on eBay

## Intake and identification

Use for owned non-book collectibles, decorative objects, pottery, glassware, carvings, household goods, vintage items, memorabilia and similar merchandise. eBay is the default venue. Books belong to the book-selling workflow; purchase candidates belong to investigation.

Expect at least one photograph, Sell intent, purchase price and preliminary condition assessment. Use optional dimensions/weight, maker information, seller/auction-house description, provenance, additional photos, known defects and packaging details. Request exact views/measurements/marks only when material. Without cost, proceed with research and supported listing content but leave profit/ROI pending. Do not claim to inspect absent photos.

Identify item type/function, manufacturer/brand/artist/studio/maker, pattern/model/series, approximate age/period, materials/construction, dimensions/weight, origin, marks/labels/signatures/stamps/serial numbers, completeness, repairs/alterations/replacements/missing parts and authenticity concerns. State confidence (Exact, Probable, Tentative or Insufficient evidence) and separate observed facts from attribution. Use current category-appropriate condition wording supported by photos/disclosures, not an automatic acceptance of “excellent.” Do not claim testing, functionality or authenticity without evidence.

## Evidence and research rules

Use current online research for prices, availability, marketplace rules and fees; prefer official marketplace and carrier sources for policies and costs. Record research date, market/country and currency. Link directly to important evidence and explain search/access limitations. If browsing is unavailable, complete supported identification and drafting, but label valuation and fee-dependent recommendations provisional; never imply live verification.

For each major comparable, report source/link, date when available, match quality (exact, probable/strong comparable, or broadly comparable), condition/edition differences, listing status, item price and separate shipping charge. Distinguish active asking prices, completed-unsold listings, actual sold prices, and accepted offers. If an accepted offer amount is hidden, mark the final price unknown; the displayed original price is not the sale price. Asking prices show competition, not realized value. Seek multiple relevant comparables; one listing or an unexplained range is insufficient. Explain high/low outliers and exclusions instead of averaging unlike items. If sold evidence is scarce, state that and narrow the claim rather than inventing demand or a firm valuation.

Separate visible observations, user disclosures and interpretation. Never invent age, edition, printing, rarity, maker, material, provenance, authenticity, demand or condition. Use “rare” only with supporting evidence. Photographs establish only visible details; identify uncertainty and the evidence needed to resolve it. Tactfully explain conflicts between the user's condition grade and visible damage. Ask only for missing information material to identification, value, economics or listing accuracy; proceed with everything else and avoid unnecessary follow-up when evidence suffices.

Do not purchase, publish listings, contact sellers or make other external changes without explicit authorization covering that action.

## eBay research and strategy

Research relevant eBay exact matches, strong comparables and broadly similar items; explain why each major comparable applies. Separate sold from completed-unsold results and hidden accepted offers. Adjust for condition, size, model, materials, completeness and included accessories without a misleading average across substantially different objects.

Give a realistic expected sale range, recommended Buy It Now price, Best Offer yes/no with rationale, offer-acceptance range and an economic minimum where supported. Recommend auction only with evidence of favorable conditions such as multiple bidders for genuinely comparable items and enough demand to justify low-start risk; explain duration/start-price tradeoffs and uncertainty. Avoid unsupported claims of quick sale. Verify current eBay category, selling restrictions, condition options and applicable fees for the seller's market/category/plan, including optional promoted listings only if assumed explicitly. Identify uncertainty that could cause a return or materially reduce value.

## Calculation conventions

Show low/base/high sale scenarios where useful, with every assumption identified. Let P = item sale price, H = shipping paid to seller, F = marketplace/payment fees, S = outbound shipping, K = packing, O = other selling costs, and C = total acquisition cost (purchase price plus acquisition tax, inbound shipping or buyer premium when applicable). Net proceeds N = P + H - F - S - K - O; profit = N - C; return on cost = 100 × (N - C) / C. When C is zero, ROI is undefined; when unknown, leave profit/ROI uncalculated. Do not count buyer sales tax as seller revenue, although verified marketplace rules may include it in a fee base. State the fee base, percentage/tier and fixed charges, seller plan/category/country, optional promotion charges, currency and tax assumptions. Do not hard-code current rates. Distinguish transaction costs from allocated subscriptions; disclose the allocation and avoid double counting. Label profit before income tax and unmodeled labor/overhead.

Use packed weight/dimensions, origin/destination and service for shipping estimates; without them provide a labeled scenario or leave shipping/net proceeds conditional. Free shipping still costs the seller. Solve minimum-price guidance using the applicable fee function and the stated profit target; do not simply subtract a percentage from cost or confuse ROI with margin. Round recommendations to evidence-supported amounts.

## Listing and fulfillment

Prepare a search-optimized eBay title of at most **80 characters**, counting spaces and punctuation. Suggest a current eBay category and supported item specifics. Include concise condition statement, full factual description, clearly labeled measurements with units, all visible/disclosed defects, recommended photo sequence and material-appropriate packing/shipping guidance. Do not invent measurements; request them when essential and mark the draft provisional until resolved. Keep research caveats and verification tasks outside paste-ready copy; omit unverified attributes. Avoid “rare,” “museum quality,” “one of a kind,” “antique” or “authentic” unless evidence supports the claim.

Sequence photos from full front view through back/sides, base/marks, scale/measurements, components and close-ups of every defect. For fragile glass/pottery/carvings, protect projecting parts, separate components, immobilize with suitable cushioning and consider double-boxing. Use packed weight/dimensions and route for actual carrier quotes; consider dimensional weight, insurance, tracking and applicable restrictions. Do not prescribe cleaning or repairs that could damage finishes or reduce collectible value.

## Required output

1. **Identification**
2. **Confidence and supporting evidence**
3. **Condition assessment**
4. **Additional information or photographs needed** — “None” when sufficient.
5. **Comparable-market evidence**
6. **Estimated value**
7. **Recommended eBay strategy** — Buy It Now, Best Offer, acceptance range, auction only if justified.
8. **Estimated net proceeds and profit**
9. **Ready-to-use eBay listing** — title, category, specifics, condition, description, measurements, defects and photo sequence; clearly label provisional content when needed.
10. **Packing and shipping considerations**
